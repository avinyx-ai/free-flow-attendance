Shift change at the two-gate entrance

Each dot is a worker. Gate 1 reads their card and face while they keep walking. By the time they reach Gate 2, the system knows whether the face matches the card, and the diverter sends them straight in or to a tripod turnstile with face recognition (Tripod FRS) for a second check.
